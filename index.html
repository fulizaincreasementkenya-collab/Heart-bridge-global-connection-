FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Who can use HeartBridge?

HeartBridge is intended for adults aged 18 and above.

Are the profiles on this page real members?

The profiles included in this template are demonstration profiles. Replace them with properly consented member profiles before presenting them as real members.

Can I apply from another country?

Yes. The country system is designed for international applications.

Where does my application go?

The website prepares your application and opens WhatsApp to the HeartBridge connection desk.

Can I contact someone directly?

Use the connection process provided by HeartBridge. The profile cards do not expose private member phone numbers.

How are profile images handled?

The production version should use images that are generated specifically for the site or supplied with appropriate permission and documentation.
